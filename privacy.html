Spin Defense Privacy Policy

Effective: November 20, 2026 · Operator: Kkabeming (individual developer) · Contact: kkabeming.world@gmail.com

This policy applies to the web game Spin Defense (https://parkbeommin.github.io/spin-defense/) and the Android app Spin Defense (io.github.parkbeommin.spindefense). The game has no accounts and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

2. Third-party services

  • Google Analytics, Google AdSense, Google AdMob, and Firebase (Cloud Firestore) are provided by Google LLC, and data may be processed on Google servers outside your country (including the United States). See policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • In the app, players in regions that require it see an ad consent screen. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Ranking and invite records are kept while the service operates and are deleted on request. Email us your ranking name and the week.
  • Game progress on your device can be removed by clearing site data in your browser, or by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
  • Retention of usage statistics and advertising data follows Google's policies.

4. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in Korea), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
